Always check Thursday injury reports before setting your lineup. Full participation means high confidence.

Weather affects passing games

FPL tips from Rigorlytics
©️
Captain the premium assets
Salah, Haaland, and Palmer consistently deliver. Only captain a differential if your mini-league demands a big rank swing.
📅
Plan for double gameweeks
Target players from teams with favorable DGW fixtures early. Having two games in a week can double your points haul.
💰
Value defenders win leagues
Budget defenders from clean sheet teams free up budget for premium midfielders and forwards that score more points.
