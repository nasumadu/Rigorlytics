What it means

Three tables, three reads.

FOR AGENTS

Know who actually has leverage.

Only 20 of 72 free agents beat their projection. Pass rush, running back and a handful of ascending names set the hot corners of the market — the rest signed at or below the number.

FOR GMs

Value pooled at the pass-catchers.

Tight end, corner and receiver all signed well under projection. Disciplined teams found starters below market while others paid the early-window premium.

FOR PLAYERS

Timing and health moved the number more than tape.

The biggest shortfalls came from injuries, unsigned waits and one-year 'prove-it' deals — not weak production. The market rewarded certainty.
