Betting intelligence principles from Rigorlytics
📐
Line movement matters
Sharp money moves lines. If a line moves against public betting, the sharps are on the other side.
🏠
Home field is overstated
Home field advantage is worth roughly 2.5-3 points in NFL. Markets often price it at 3+ giving away value.
🎲
Props over spreads
Player props offer more statistical edge than spreads. Books are sharper on game lines than individual player markets.
📅
Injury reports are gold
Late injury news creates market inefficiency. A key player ruled out after lines are set is often the best betting opportunity.
