Front office principles from Rigorlytics
📉
Pay for the future
Contracts should price projected production over the deal term, not past performance that may not repeat.
🕐
Age curves matter
Most athletes peak between 26 and 29. Long deals signed at 30 plus carry significant decline risk.
💵
Surplus value wins
Championship teams are built on players producing above their salary, not on paying market rate for stars.
🔄
Flexibility is an asset
Cap space and tradeable contracts have real option value. Avoid deals that eliminate future maneuverability.
