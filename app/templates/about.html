What is Rigorlytics

Rigorlytics is an AI-powered sports intelligence platform built to transform how people analyze, understand, and make decisions in sports.

Every roster move, draft selection, contract negotiation, and game strategy is driven by data, and that intelligence should be accessible to everyone, not just those with expensive tools and proprietary databases. Rigorlytics provides the context behind the numbers, not just the numbers themselves.

We're building a comprehensive sports intelligence ecosystem that supports fans, analysts, coaches, media professionals, students, and sports executives across multiple leagues and sports.

The story
Bringing GM mode
into the real world

I earned my MBA from Virginia Tech and my B.S. in Information Systems from VCU. Rigorlytics was born from my love for sports and my belief that technology and AI can fundamentally improve decision-making in the sports world.

Business decisions in sports carry enormous weight. A single move can elevate a franchise for a decade or set it back just as long. That reality has always fascinated me.

Growing up, I spent countless hours in GM mode, building rosters and making the calls that shape a team's future. Rigorlytics is my attempt to bring that same experience into the real world, giving scouts, GMs, executives, and front offices a tool that helps them make better decisions and win.
